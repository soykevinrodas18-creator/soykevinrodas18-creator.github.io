THE VOYAGE

Step Aboard the Thousand Sunny

Some restaurants serve dinner. This one drops anchor in another world.

Past the doors on S Dixie Hwy, the dining room turns into the Grand Line — warm wood like a ship's hull, pirate flags overhead, wanted posters on the walls, a towering Zoro greeting the crew at the entrance, and shelves of figurines collected like treasure.

Every dish is named for the voyage: ramen worthy of the Pirate King, sizzling skillets fit for a Yonko, boba teas poured for the whole Straw Hat crew. Come for the food, stay for the adventure — dine in, take out, or get it delivered.

"It's not just about the food — it's the adventure. Every corner is crafted to make you feel like a true Straw Hat pirate."

THE CREW

The People Behind the Ship

The Thousand Sunny is the second voyage of the team behind Naruto 88 Bistro — the anime-obsessed crew, led by co-owner Bin Chen, that turned a Kendall ramen spot into a home port for Miami's anime fans. For their next chapter they sailed to Pinecrest with a love letter to One Piece: a restaurant where the food hits as hard as the fandom.

BIN CHEN & CREWCO-OWNER · NARUTO 88 BISTRO TEAM

The same hands, the same obsession with the details — from the wanted posters on the walls to the names on the menu. If you have eaten at their Kendall spot, you already know the crew. This is their bigger ship.
