The artist

Photographs freeze a moment. Paintings hold the feeling.

I'm Whitney Paige, a fine artist specializing in live event painting. I arrive at your venue hours before your guests, sketch your ceremony or reception scene, and spend the evening bringing it to life — brushstroke by brushstroke — while everyone watches.

I also offer live watercolor portraits for your guests: a personal, one-of-a-kind keepsake they take home the same night. It's the detail your guests talk about long after the last dance.

Every painting is finished in my studio with gold-leaf accents and delivered ready to hang — a museum-worthy heirloom of the best day of your life.

How it works

Three steps to a painting you'll cry over.

Step 01

I arrive & sketch

I set up discreetly hours before your event and sketch the scene you chose — your ceremony, first dance, or venue — along with your color palette and floral details.

Step 02

I paint live

Throughout your celebration, guests gather around the easel and watch your painting emerge in real time. It becomes live entertainment and a memory in the making.

Step 03

You take it home

After final studio touches and gold-leaf detailing, your finished painting is delivered gallery-wrapped and ready to hang — the centerpiece of your home forever.
