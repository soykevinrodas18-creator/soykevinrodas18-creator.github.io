The artist

Photographs freeze a moment. Paintings hold the feeling.

I'm Whitney Paige, a fine artist specializing in live event painting. I arrive at your venue before your guests, sketch the scene you've chosen, and spend the evening bringing it to life — brushstroke by brushstroke — while everyone watches.

I also paint live watercolor portraits for your guests: a personal, one-of-a-kind keepsake they take home the very same night. It's the detail your guests will talk about long after the last dance.

The Cherished Rain Philosophy

How it works

Three steps to a painting you'll cry over.

Step I

I arrive & sketch

I set up discreetly before your event and sketch the scene you chose — your ceremony, first dance, or venue — along with your color palette and floral details.

Step II

I paint live

Throughout your celebration, guests gather around the easel and watch your painting emerge in real time. It becomes living entertainment — and a memory in the making.

Step III

You take it home

After final studio refinement, your finished painting is delivered gallery-wrapped and ready to hang — the centerpiece of your home, forever.
