The House

Not a cheese shop.
A temple of taste.

3 Locations
Miami

Chèvre — French for goat — is Miami's love letter to the European fromagerie. Walk in for a wheel of Vacherin Mont d'Or, stay for a glass of wine, leave with a bag full of good decisions.

Every shelf is curated, not stocked. From 18-month prosciutto di San Daniele DOP to the Rothschild Truffle Brie that sells out every time it lands, only the best ingredients make it through the door.

And then there's the café — where a humble burger became a cult-favorite phenomenon, and the grilled cheese earned the title "world famous" from the people who know.

The Curator

Behind every great house
is a great palate.

Mario D Naar — known to Miami as @naarmiami — is the wine lover and master curator behind Chèvre. The bio says it plainly: "Curated by @naarmiami."

"Content isn't king — it's the Kingdom."

Curation is his craft. Beyond Chèvre, Mario curates a small constellation of Miami concepts — each one built the same way: obsessive sourcing, honest flavor, and an experience worth talking about. That's why the shelves read like a collector's shelf, and why a cheese shop ended up with a cult-favorite burger.
