The man behind the flat-top

Meet Ted

I just kid — we make some absolute gas out here, and that’s a promise.

Ted is the first voice you hear on every post. He signs every caption himself — “Much love ❤️ -Ted” — because Ted’s Burgers was never a brand first. It was a guy, a backyard, and a flat-top that wouldn’t quit.

He calls himself “this little dude trying to make it happen,” but the neighborhood knows better: sneaker raffles in-store, a 4th of July burger-and-wings eating contest, collabs with Miami food royalty, and a line out the door seven days a week.

Come have a burger — he’ll promise to make you smile.
