A tasting, course by course

Omakase means trusting the chef. At OMAKAI, the menu unfolds the way a kaiseki dinner does — one precise course at a time, from the first warm grain of rice to the last crisp bite of nori.

The hand roll bar

Crisp nori, eaten now
