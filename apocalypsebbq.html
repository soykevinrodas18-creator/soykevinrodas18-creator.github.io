The man behind the fire

"F*ck the apoca­lypse.
Let's BBQ."

One sentence, said out loud in a driveway in 2020, became the most-followed BBQ joint in Miami.

Jeff Budnechky wasn't a pitmaster. When the pandemic shut everything down, he had no work, too much time, and a backyard smoker. So he created the @apocalypsebbq handle on a whim, registered the name, and started calling his friends.

The first week he sold two racks of ribs. Then five. Then ten. And it never stopped growing. His wife Laura — and her mother Aida — jumped on the ride, and a backyard hobby turned into one of the top-rated barbecue restaurants in Miami, now home in Kendall with a second location on the way.

Behind the pits stands pitmaster Aaron, running a blend of hickory and oak that gives every bite its depth. No shortcuts, no gas — just wood, smoke, patience, and Miami attitude.

"We all stand behind our cornbread."— The Apocalypse Crew

Today the fire spreads further: the Bad Company chef dinner series, collabs with Miami's best, and Lala's — the smash-burger offshoot named after Laura herself. Family first, smoke always.
