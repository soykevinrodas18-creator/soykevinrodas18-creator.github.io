Why Ernie's

The person behind it

Meet Hernando.

Ernie's began in 2022 on a business trip to Tempe, Arizona, where founder Hernando Caicedo discovered a soft-serve açaí concept — and saw exactly what Miami was missing: a spot built around açaí itself.

He came home, refined the idea in-house, and built a proprietary 100% organic blend — lower sugar, no added water, creamier and denser than anything in town. No bank backing, no paid influencers, no PR. Just word of mouth and a better bowl.

Today Ernie's is Miami's homegrown açaí staple, run alongside partner Andrew Platt and a team that treats every swirl like it matters — from Coral Gables to Parkland.

"Eat at Ernie's. Try a bowl that's different from any you've ever had — you'll be glad you did." Hernando Caicedo — President & Co-Founder
