We pull them. Every installation we do is permitted and inspected — panel upgrades, EV charging, service work, all of it. Unpermitted electrical work voids insurance and kills property sales; we don’t do it, and we don’t recommend anyone who does.

NEC 625.42 allows EV charging loads to be managed by an energy management system instead of sized at full nameplate — which often means you can add chargers without a costly service upgrade. We design EMS per 625.42 and 750.30 so your project passes inspection and your panel doesn’t need to be twice the size.

Usually, yes — that’s our specialty. We start with a load study of the building’s service, then design a load-managed charger layout that fits the existing capacity. Most multifamily garages we assess can support EV charging with an EMS instead of a service upgrade.

Assessments and quotes are delivered within 48 hours. A panel upgrade typically runs 1–2 days of on-site work once permits are issued; Miami-Dade permitting usually adds 1–3 weeks. Multi-charger EV projects are phased — we give you a real schedule in the engineered plan, before you sign anything.
