Our Story

From a Venezuelan kitchen, to Miami's table.

The Oats Guy started with one simple idea: family recipes, rebuilt around oats — the humble grain that makes every bite hearty, honest and deeply satisfying.

Every cake, cookie and ring is baked in small batches, by hand, in our Miami micro bakery. No shortcuts — just clean ingredients and recipes passed down with love.

"Healthy & artisanal desserts made with love — and the cleanest ingredients possible."
