The artisan behind the scoop

A garage, a dream, and one unforgettable recipe.

Danielle Gelato carries on a tradition born in 1978, when a 19-year-old named Daniel Paradiso opened a tiny gelateria in a rented garage in Victoria, Buenos Aires. He had learned the craft as a boy, working summers in a neighborhood heladería since he was twelve — and he never stopped perfecting it.

1978 — Victoria, Argentina

Daniel opens his first heladería in a garage on Palacios street, crafting every batch by hand.

1985 — La Horqueta

The first branch opens. Word of an extraordinary dulce de leche starts to travel.

Today — Miami

That same artisan spirit lives on in Coconut Grove and Little River, where every pan is still made fresh daily.

From natural colors to seasonal flavors, true gelato is creamy, rich, and crafted with passion — not fluff.
