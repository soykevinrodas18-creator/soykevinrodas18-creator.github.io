04Process

Prep is everything.

The seamless finish isn't luck — it's a process. Every vehicle goes through the same ritual before a single inch of film touches the paint.

STEP — 01

Deep Prep

In-depth wash and decontamination. Iron removal, clay, and a surgically clean surface — because film only performs on perfect paint.

STEP — 02

Paint Correction

Machine polishing to remove swirls and defects. We lock in perfection before we protect it.

STEP — 03

Precision Install

PPF, tint or coating applied with relief cuts and edge work that disappears. No tension, no seams, no shortcuts.

STEP — 04

Final Inspection

Panel-by-panel quality check under proper lighting. It doesn't leave until it's divine.
